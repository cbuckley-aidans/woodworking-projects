Marking out the pilot holes

All positions are measured from the bottom left corner of each face, looking at the outside face of that part. Every pilot hole is 2.5 mm.

Square against one edge, mark the line, then step the measurements along it.

Front panel: drilling the entrance and dowel holes

Assembly

Work in this order. The part named in each step is the one clamped in the vice, and screws are always driven into its edge. Hole labels match the marking out diagrams above.

1Floor to back

In the vice: Floor (D)

Grip the floor in the vice with one 140 mm edge facing up. Stand the back against that edge, bottom edges flush, and drive two screws through A1 and A2 into the floor edge.

Pilot holes used: A1 and A2 on the back.

2Sides to back

In the vice: Side (E), one at a time

Grip a side in the vice with its 160 mm back edge facing up. Lay the back across it, flush at the corner, and drive two screws through the back into that edge. Repeat for the second side.

Pilot holes used: A3 to A6 on the back, two per side.

3Front on

In the vice: The part built box, held by the back panel

Grip the assembly in the vice so the front edges of the sides and floor face up. Sit the front in place and drive six screws: four into the side edges, two into the floor edge.

Pilot holes used: B3 to B6 into the sides, B1 and B2 into the floor.

4Roof last

In the vice: The assembled box, held by the back panel

Grip the box so the sloped top edges of the sides face up. Sit the roof on so its back edge meets the back panel, then drive four screws. No glue, so the roof lifts off for cleaning.

Pilot holes used: C1 to C4 on the roof.
