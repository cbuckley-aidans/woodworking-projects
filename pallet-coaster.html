Cutting layout

Each saw cut removes about 1.5 mm, so always measure the next 15 mm from the freshly cut end rather than marking all five strips at once.

Cutting the slats

The strips are cut across the board, so the grain runs across each slat's 15 mm width. Handle the strips gently, as they can snap along the grain.
